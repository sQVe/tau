`slice` adds a Mermaid flowchart of the `blocked-by` edges to its preview when the slices branch or
join. The `pr` fallback body allows one diagram under Decisions when the PR changes how parts
connect. The `explain` snippet asks for a diagram when the change alters how parts connect, and the
`simplify` snippet asks for diagrams of the current and the proposed design.
