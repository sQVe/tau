Build and test Tau against Pi 0.99.0. Pi now supplies `@earendil-works/pi-ai`,
`@earendil-works/pi-coding-agent`, `@earendil-works/pi-tui`, and `typebox`, so Tau lists them as
peer dependencies with a `*` range instead of installing its own copies. This removes Pi's launch
warning about host-provided packages in `dependencies`.
