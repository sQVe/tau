The brainstorm, tracker, slice, and pr skills open tuicr beside each draft approval and collect the
review after the answer, instead of offering a "Review in tuicr" option. Accept with only edits
applies them and accepts. Accept with a comment, or Request changes, revises the draft and asks
again. `draft_review` no longer has the blocking `review` action; use `open` and `collect`.
