# Human ticket

Use this shape for work a person reads that is not a design, a slice, or a bug. Leave out optional
sections that have nothing to say.

```markdown
One to three sentences on what changes and why, for a person who scans the ticket.

## Problem

Optional. What is wrong or missing today, and who it affects.

## Out of scope

Optional. Work a reader might expect here that this ticket leaves out.

## Acceptance

- [ ] An observable result that shows the work is done.
```
