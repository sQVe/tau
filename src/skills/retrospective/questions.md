# Retrospective questions

Pick the questions the task needs. Each example names what it may need. Read only that evidence.
These examples guide inquiry. They are not a checklist, and a finding outside them is valid.

## How to read answers

- Leave out a phase the task did not need. Do not list it as a gap or a failure.
- Look into a missing phase only when a concrete observed consequence makes it relevant.
- A rule that was followed does not show the work was effective. Record the observed result.
- A suspicion can be refuted. Keep the refutation with its evidence.
- Report a finding that spans several workflows once, under its own title. List each workflow's
  evidence under that title.

## Intent and scope

- Which uncertainty did research settle before a decision? Did the record show what was known then?
- Where did scope or acceptance change, and what happened next?
- Did a worker get the context, acceptance criteria, and constraints it needed? What did its report
  show it lacked?
- Did the worker's result match the acceptance criteria it was given?

## Execution and confidence

- What did the reproduction or failing test establish? Did a later result change that conclusion?
- Which problems did review or QA find, and in which round? What evidence existed before that round?
- Did a check fail? How was the failure handled, and was the handling recorded?

## Continuity and delivery

- What evidence supported a completion claim when it was made? Did later evidence support or
  contradict it?
- What shows whether a publication or handover succeeded, such as a saved send result?
- What context survived a session change or handover? What did the receiver lack?

## Cross-cutting

- Which user decisions affected the work? Was the information needed to decide available?
- Which repository, tool, or environment constraint changed the path of the work? How did the agent
  recover?
- Where did project instructions help, conflict, or add friction? Judge them by the observed effect,
  not by the rule text alone.
- Did a skill step cause rework or an unclear decision? Name the step.
- What differed between the user's intent and the observed outcome?
- What did delegation preserve or lose in worker reports?
- Which evidence or context was missing, stale, or misunderstood?
- What worked well enough to repeat? What evidence supports that conclusion?
