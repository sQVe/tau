Drive this to the end without waiting for me, and ask me no questions. At each decision, pick your recommended option and record it with the reason. Treat this as my approval to push, and to open or update the PR. Stop only for what you cannot do yourself, such as a login, a secret, or a merge. At the end, list the decisions you made and what is left for me.
