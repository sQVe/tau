---

Demo this in a new herdr-app pane to the right of yours, so I can watch it run. Find your pane ID in `herdr-app ls`, where it lists your `HC_TERMINAL_ID`, and run the demo with `herdr-app do pane.split --direction right --pane <your pane> --argv '[...]'`. If you cannot find your pane, ask me. Tell me what to look at, and leave the pane open when you finish.
