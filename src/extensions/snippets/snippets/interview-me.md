name: Interview me
description: Read the code and docs, then ask me until the work is clear
placement: prepend
order: 10
---

Interview me before you start. Read the code and docs first to understand what you can without asking me. Then ask me about anything that is still unclear. Ask at most four questions per round. If a question depends on an earlier answer, wait for that answer before asking it. Keep asking until the goal, scope, and expected result are clear to you. Summarize what we agreed on. Do not start the work until I say we agree.
