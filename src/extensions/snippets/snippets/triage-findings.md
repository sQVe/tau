---
name: Triage findings
description: Judge each finding, act on the real ones, say why the rest are out
placement: append
order: 30
---

Treat the findings as claims to verify, not a task list. Verify each one against the code before you act on it. Act on the findings that hold. For the rest, say in one line why you are leaving them. Separate the findings that are wrong from the ones that are real but not worth the change.
