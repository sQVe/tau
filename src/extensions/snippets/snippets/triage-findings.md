---
name: Triage findings
description: Judge each finding, fix the real ones, say why the rest are out
placement: append
order: 30
---

Treat the findings as claims to check, not a task list. Confirm each one against the code before you
act on it. Fix the ones that hold. For the rest, say in one line why you are leaving them, and
separate the ones that are wrong from the ones that are real but not worth the change.
